# Feature List — Halal Chiang Rai (Phase: DISCOVER)

- **[Core] Search & View Halal Restaurant**: Search and view halal restaurants around you via Map View / Card List View, with filters for name, cuisine type, and price, and results ranked to match your preferences (from US01, US03, US05)
- **Zone & Area Discovery**: Find restaurants in popular zones (around MFU, Chiang Rai City Center), limited to results within the selected district (from US02)
- **Halal Classification & Verification**: Distinct badges separating "Halal Certified" vs. "Muslim-Friendly", with verifiable accreditation proof (from US04, US07)
- **Restaurant Details & Amenities**: Show menu, pricing, opening hours, parking, and prayer spaces (from US06, US08)
- **Facilities, Favorites & Trip Planning**: Find mosques/musalla, bookmark favorite places, and plan multi-stop trips (from US09, US10)
- **Community & Merchant Portal**: Rate and review, report incorrect information, and let owners manage their own listings (from US11, US12, US13)
